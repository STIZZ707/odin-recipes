Street Tacos

Description

This simple street taco recipe is delicious and fast. Bring a quick taste of Mexican street food right to your kitchen table for a very affordable price.

Ingredients

  • A pack of corn tortillas
  • A bunch of cilantro
  • Three limes
  • Your desired amount of skirt steak or sirloin, but any meat will do
  • One white onion
  • A taco seasoning blend, or just salt, pepper, cumin, and garlic powder

Steps

  1. Season your steak and place into a plastic bag with the juice of one of your limes. Let sit for up to 30 minutes, but no longer.
  2. While your steak is marinating, dice your onion and chop the cilantro.
  3. Turn on your grill to high heat and allow to get hot.
  4. Once your grill is hot, remove your steak and place directly on the grill.
  5. With your steak cooking, slice your remaining limes into clean fourths or desired thickness.
  6. Make sure to flip your steak regularly, you are looking for a good char on it!
  7. Your steak should take about 15 minutes and once ready, remove to cool for a few minutes.
  8. While cooling, place your tortillas directly on the grill until warm.
  9. After resting for 5-10 minutes, slice your steak into strips. Be sure to go against the grain.
  10. Now lay your tortillas out and top with a few slices of steak. Add your onions and cilantro on top.
  11. Garnish with a few lime slices and enjoy your street tacos!
